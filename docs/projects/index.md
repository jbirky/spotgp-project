# Projects

Here is a list of proposed projects that I'm interested in working on using `spotgp`.  

To add your project, copy [`_template.md`](https://github.com/jbirky/spotgp-project/blob/main/docs/projects/_template.md), fill it in, and open a pull request.

---


## Benchmark Systems

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **The Sun as a Star**

    ---

    Can we infer differential rotation and changes in spot properties at different phases of the Sun's activity cycle?


-   **Simulated data: Hare-and-Hounds Recovery Test**

    ---

    How well we can recover ground-truth parameters from simulated lightcurves with Kepler systematics and noise?

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: View project](hare-and-hounds.md)


-   **Transit-mapped stars**

    ---

    How do the spot properties inferred from `spotgp` compare to exoplanet hosts with individual spot properties measured from transit mapping?

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: View project](transit-mapping.md)


-   **Asteroseismic constraints: 16 Cygni A & B**

    ---

    How do measurements from `spotgp` compare to benchmark targets with interferometric radii measurements, and asteroseismic masses, ages, inclinations and differential rotation measurements?

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: View project](16-cygni.md)

</div>
