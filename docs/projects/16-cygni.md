# 16 Cygni A & B 

## Abstract


16 Cyg A and B are well-characterized solar analogues observed by Kepler:
interferometric radii, asteroseismic masses and ages, asteroseismic
inclinations, and asteroseismic differential rotation for *both* components, 
making it a very good independent benchmark for testing the consistency 
of parameters derived with `spotgp`.
